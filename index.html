BILLY PATCHANON OUNSA-ARD

Actor · Artist · Model

Billy Patchanon Ounsa-ard is a Thai actor and artist known for his captivating screen presence, versatile acting skills, and distinctive charm. Through his dedication and passion, Billy continues to explore new opportunities across the entertainment and fashion industries, earning the love and support of fans in Thailand and around the world.

Throughout his career, Billy has gained recognition for his performances in various television series, including Secret Crush on You, War of Y, The Sign, and Interminable. Each project showcases his growth as an actor and his ability to bring different characters to life.

Beyond acting, Billy continues to expand his presence through international fan events, fashion appearances, and other professional opportunities. His journey reflects his commitment to his craft and his continuous pursuit of new achievements.
